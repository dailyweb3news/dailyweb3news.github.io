Gnosis Bridge: Which transfer steps to double-check

Gnosis Bridge transfer refresher: check the route, source network and gas, then claim Ethereum withdrawals and confirm the destination balance on an explorer.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 23, 2026 · 19:50 UTC3 min read

For a Gnosis Bridge transfer refresher, double-check the token route, source network and gas, and leave time and ETH for an Ethereum claim. The Gnosis Bridge is where you start a Gnosis token bridge transfer; this refresher adds the checks that help you finish with a visible, confirmed balance. The steps vary by token and direction: DAI uses the xDai Bridge and arrives as native xDAI, while other ERC-20 tokens use Omnibridge.

How do you complete a Gnosis Bridge transfer?

Start with tokens in a wallet on the source network. Have MetaMask set up with Ethereum and Gnosis Chain, ETH for Ethereum gas, and a small xDAI balance for gas on Gnosis Chain.

  1. Open the official bridge and connect MetaMask. Choose whether you are moving tokens to Gnosis Chain or Ethereum.
  2. Choose the token and amount. The route depends on the token: DAI moves through the xDai Bridge and arrives as native xDAI; other ERC-20 tokens move through Omnibridge. Review the selected route before continuing.
  3. Confirm MetaMask is on the source network. Sign on Ethereum when sending from Ethereum, or on Gnosis Chain when sending from Gnosis Chain. MetaMask must show the source network before you approve or submit.
  4. Approve the token, if prompted. Approval lets the bridge contract use the token. It is separate from the transfer, so approving alone does not move your funds.
  5. Submit the deposit. Confirm the second transaction in MetaMask. This is the transaction that moves the tokens. The gas currency depends on the source: ETH on Ethereum, xDAI on Gnosis Chain.
  6. Wait for bridge validators to confirm. Validators are the parties that sign off on the cross-chain transfer. For a withdrawal to Ethereum, keep ETH in the receiving address before you start: after validators sign, you must submit a claim transaction on Ethereum, which also costs ETH.
  7. Confirm the destination balance. Complete the Ethereum claim if withdrawing there, then add the bridged token’s contract to your wallet if its balance is not displayed. Find the transfer on GnosisScan or Etherscan and confirm the destination transaction and balance.

Related questions

How long does a Gnosis Bridge transfer take?

It depends on the direction and validator confirmation. An Ethereum withdrawal also needs the separate claim transaction, so it is not complete when validators sign.

Can I cancel a Gnosis Bridge transfer?

Do not assume a submitted transfer can be cancelled. Check its status on the relevant explorer before taking another action.

Gnosis Bridge questions

What is the minimum amount Gnosis Bridge accepts?

The minimum can depend on the token and route. Check the amount accepted by the bridge for your selected transfer before signing; no single minimum is established here.

Can I bridge to a different receiving address?

Use a different address only if the transfer form offers that option. Confirm the address and that you control it before submitting.

Why does my bridged token have a different name on Gnosis Chain?

DAI sent through the xDai Bridge arrives as native xDAI. Other tokens can have a different display name in a wallet; use the token contract and explorer record to identify the asset.

Is Gnosis Bridge cheaper than Jumper or Bungee?

There is no fixed answer without comparing the same token, direction and amount at the time you transfer, including network gas. Check the current total for each option before choosing.
