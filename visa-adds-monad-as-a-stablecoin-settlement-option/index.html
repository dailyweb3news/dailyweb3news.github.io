Visa adds Monad as a stablecoin settlement option

Visa has added Monad as a blockchain option for stablecoin settlement, giving participating issuers and acquirers another route to settle payment obligations.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 11, 2026 · 20:42 UTC2 min read

Visa has added the Monad blockchain as an option for stablecoin settlement, giving participating card issuers and acquirers another way to settle payment obligations. The Monad Foundation announced the change on November 10, saying the network would be available for settlement through Visa’s program. A Monad Foundation announcement said the option uses a permissionless network, meaning access is not limited to approved users.

Visa’s Dan Roesbery said the addition gives partners more choice over which blockchain networks they use. The Block also reported that Visa’s participating issuers and acquirers can now settle through Monad. Stablecoins are digital tokens designed to hold a steady value, often against a currency such as the US dollar.

How could settlement on Monad change the timing?

It could let funds move outside the usual weekday banking schedule. The Monad Foundation said card settlement has traditionally followed a five-business-day schedule over banking rails, while blockchain settlement can run on weekends and holidays. The Block reported the same point, citing the foundation.

The change concerns settlement between payment firms. It does not mean cardholders will need to use Monad directly. The foundation said the Visa experience for merchants and cardholders would remain familiar, with settlement happening behind the scenes.

The announcement did not name any issuers or acquirers that will use Monad, or identify a specific stablecoin for settlement. It also gave no date for when individual partners might begin using the option.

What privacy feature is planned?

Monad says it is developing a feature called Private Settlement for institutions that need to keep sensitive transaction details confidential. The foundation said the feature is expected in the coming months and would still let institutions share details with auditors and regulators.

The planned feature is being built into the network, according to the foundation. It said private transactions would remain connected to the network’s liquidity. The Block reported those details and noted that the privacy feature is still in development.

For now, the confirmed change is that Monad has joined Visa’s available settlement networks. The announcement does not say how much settlement will take place on Monad or whether the network will change the overall volume Visa handles.
