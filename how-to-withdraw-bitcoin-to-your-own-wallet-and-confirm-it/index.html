How to withdraw Bitcoin to your own wallet and confirm it

To withdraw bitcoin from an exchange to your own wallet, create a wallet, back up its seed phrase, send BTC on Bitcoin, then track the txid to confirmation.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 25, 2026 · 03:31 UTC3 min read

When BTC is sitting in an exchange account, withdraw it by creating a wallet, backing up its seed phrase, sending to its Bitcoin receive address and tracking the txid until the balance appears.

A wallet and seed backup come before withdrawal

To move BTC into your control, first set up a wallet whose keys you hold and secure its seed phrase offline. A seed phrase is usually 12 or 24 words; write it down and keep it private before copying any receive address. Have your exchange 2FA ready for the withdrawal confirmation.

A personal wallet is also the starting point for moving Bitcoin toward DeFi. The RenBridge replacement for Bitcoin to Ethereum is a useful case of a cross-chain bridge moving value between chains. RenBridge is discontinued, and today that route points to bridges such as tBTC.

Send BTC on the Bitcoin network and track confirmation

To withdraw BTC, use the wallet’s Bitcoin receive address in the exchange’s BTC withdrawal form and confirm the transfer.

  1. Create and back up the wallet. Set up a wallet, write its seed phrase offline, and keep it somewhere private. The wallet is ready to receive BTC.
  2. Copy the receive address. Open the wallet’s Bitcoin receive details and copy the address. Bitcoin addresses commonly start with 1, 3, bc1q or bc1p.
  3. Open the exchange withdrawal form. Choose BTC and select the Bitcoin network. Paste the wallet address, then compare its first and last characters with the address shown in the wallet.
  4. Verify on the hardware wallet screen. Clipboard malware can replace a copied address on your computer. The hardware wallet’s own display is outside that computer’s reach, so compare the full address shown there with the withdrawal address before sending. This catches a swap that checking only the first and last characters could miss.
  5. Enter the amount and confirm 2FA. Review the amount, address and Bitcoin network, then approve the withdrawal with your exchange’s 2FA. Choosing another network, such as BEP20, sends a token version rather than BTC; an address may not be able to hold it.
  6. Track the txid. The exchange provides a transaction ID, or txid, for the withdrawal. Look it up on a Bitcoin block explorer. When the transaction has confirmations and the balance appears in your wallet, the BTC has arrived. The exchange balance has gone down; the same BTC is now visible in the wallet you control.

Related

How long does a Bitcoin withdrawal from an exchange take?

A Bitcoin withdrawal is complete when it has confirmations and appears in your wallet. The txid lets you follow the transaction on a block explorer while it is pending.

Why does an exchange set a minimum Bitcoin withdrawal?

An exchange minimum sets the smallest BTC amount it will process in a withdrawal. Check the amount shown in the withdrawal form before confirming.
