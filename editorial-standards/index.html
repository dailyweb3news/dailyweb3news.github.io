Editorial standards

What we cover

Onchain Brief covers the Crypto industry as news: market structure, protocol changes, regulation, funding and security. Stories describe events and their context, not where prices are headed.

How we source stories

We work from primary material wherever it exists — company and project statements, regulatory documents, on-chain records — and from reputable reporting. Sources are linked in the text so readers can check them.

Editorial independence

What we cover and how we frame it is an editorial decision. Naming a company, protocol or asset in a story does not mean we endorse it.

Fixing mistakes

Accuracy matters more than speed. When an error of fact comes to light, the article is corrected.

Not financial advice

Everything on Onchain Brief is published for general information. It is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Crypto assets are volatile; do your own research before making any decision.
