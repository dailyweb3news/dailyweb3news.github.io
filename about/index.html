About Onchain Brief

Onchain Brief reports news from across crypto: Bitcoin and Ethereum, DeFi, stablecoins, exchanges, wallets, security incidents, regulation and new networks. Each story says what happened, who it affects and what is still unclear.

We are independent and not tied to any project, token or exchange. We work from primary sources such as official statements, filings, code repositories and onchain data, and we name them. When we get something wrong, we fix it and add a note to the story. Nothing here is financial advice.
