How do you redeem LP tokens on Fraxswap?

To redeem LP tokens for underlying assets, choose the matching pool, enter an amount, review both token minimums, and confirm the withdrawal in your wallet.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 17, 2026 · 13:22 UTC3 min read

Open Fraxswap on the network holding your LP tokens, because the network and pair determine which pool position you can withdraw. To redeem LP tokens, select the matching pool, enter an amount to remove, review the estimated tokens and minimums, then confirm the withdrawal in your wallet; the pool burns the LP tokens and sends the underlying assets to that wallet.

  • Have the pair’s LP tokens and the network’s native gas token in your connected wallet.
  • Review the estimated amount and minimum for each underlying token before confirming.
  • After confirmation, check the transaction status and token balances in your wallet.

LP tokens are receipts for a share of one specific token pair. Before starting, match the pair and network to the LP tokens in your wallet. For a closer look at Fraxswap LP claims on pool assets, keep in mind that Fraxswap pair contracts hold the pool’s assets while the Router handles the withdrawal.

The pool returns its share of both tokens

Say a wallet redeems 10% of a pool’s LP tokens. The withdrawal returns the corresponding share of each token held by that pool when the transaction executes. The amounts depend on the pool balances at that moment, so they can change while a transaction is waiting to be confirmed.

Fraxswap is a permissionless automated market maker built on a Uniswap V2 core. Its Router removes liquidity by burning the LP tokens and returning the pair’s underlying tokens. The pool share is split across both assets; withdrawing liquidity does not let you choose to receive only one of them.

In the pool’s remove-liquidity view, select the pair and enter an LP token amount or percentage. Review the estimates for both tokens, along with each minimum amount. Those minimums are floors for the withdrawal: they tell the transaction the least of each token you will accept.

Consider two withdrawals with the same LP amount, pool, and minimums. In the first, the pool balances stay steady until confirmation, so both outputs meet their minimums and the withdrawal can complete. In the second, balances move before confirmation and one output falls below its minimum. The transaction reverts rather than paying less than that floor. You keep the LP tokens, but the failed transaction can still use gas.

Submit the withdrawal and confirm it in your wallet. Once the transaction is confirmed, check its receipt or status and look for both tokens in your wallet. Token balances may need to be displayed in the wallet before they are easy to spot.

Why did my liquidity removal fail?

Check that your wallet is on the correct network, holds the pair’s LP tokens, and has enough native gas token. If the transaction reverted, one or both outputs may have fallen below the minimums after pool balances changed.

  • Review the estimated outputs and minimums for both tokens.
  • Confirm in your wallet, then check the transaction and token balances.
