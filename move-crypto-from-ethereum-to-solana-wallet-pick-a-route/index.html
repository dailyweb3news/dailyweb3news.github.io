Move Crypto from Ethereum to Solana Wallet: Pick a Route

How to move crypto from Ethereum to a Solana wallet: bridge supported tokens or withdraw on Solana, then confirm the token mint before spending.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 28, 2026 · 04:21 UTC3 min read

To move crypto from Ethereum to your Solana wallet, use a bridge that supports both chains or withdraw from an exchange on Solana; the chains are separate, so you cannot send directly between them. For USDC, the USDC bridge article explains the cross-chain route, and Circle’s supported-chain list confirms CCTP supports Ethereum and Solana.

How do I move crypto from Ethereum to my Solana wallet?

Choose a bridge or exchange route, then send to your Solana address. A wallet such as Phantom may show both Ethereum and Solana, but the assets remain on separate chains. The Ethereum address starts with 0x; a Solana address uses base58 characters. Copy the Solana address from your wallet and make sure the route’s destination network is Solana.

  • Bridge: Connect both wallets, choose Ethereum as the source and Solana as the destination, then select the token and amount.
  • Exchange: Deposit the asset on Ethereum, then withdraw it to your Solana address with Solana selected as the withdrawal network.

What do I need before sending?

Have ETH in your Ethereum wallet for gas, your Solana wallet address, and a small SOL balance in that wallet. The SOL pays Solana transaction fees and can fund the token account needed to hold a token. Solana’s [token-account and rent documentation](https://solana.com/developers/docs/core/rent) explains that a new token account needs a small refundable storage deposit. You are ready when your wallet can cover fees and the route shows the correct Solana address.

Will my token arrive as native or wrapped?

ETH and most ERC-20 tokens arrive as wrapped tokens: Solana versions that represent assets from another chain. For USDC, choose a route using Circle CCTP to receive native USDC on Solana. Its mint address is EPjFWdd5AufqSSqeM2qN1xzybapC8G4wEGGkZwyTDt1v.

Some routes may deliver wrapped USDCet instead. Compare the output token’s mint address with the one you expect before confirming; the same ticker alone does not identify the asset. With other tokens, consider swapping to USDC before bridging or receiving SOL if you need Solana’s native token.

What happens after I approve the transfer?

Approve the token allowance and send the transaction on Ethereum, then wait for the bridge to process it. Some routes, including Wormhole Portal transfers, may need a separate redeem transaction on Solana before the tokens appear. Wormhole’s [wrapped-token transfer documentation](https://wormhole.com/docs/products/token-transfers/wrapped-token-transfers/guides/transfer-wrapped-assets/) describes manual redemption on the destination chain. Keep enough SOL available to submit that step.

What is the cheapest way to move crypto to Solana, and can I bridge ETH and receive SOL?

The cheapest route depends on the token, amount, bridge or exchange fees, and Ethereum gas at the time. Compare the final amount received and the output token, not just the quoted fee. You can bridge ETH and receive SOL only when the route explicitly offers a swap into SOL; otherwise, ETH arrives as a wrapped token. Check the final Solana balance and mint address to confirm the transfer completed as intended.
