Repaying a DeFi loan starts with the borrowed token

How to repay a DeFi loan step by step: hold the borrowed token, approve a small buffer, then repay a set amount or max and confirm your balance falls.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 25, 2026 · 16:46 UTC3 min read

After opening your lending dashboard, repay a DeFi loan by selecting the borrowed asset, approving it, and confirming a partial amount or max. Your debt is tracked in that asset, so you need the same token on the same network: DAI debt takes DAI, not USDS. Have the borrowing wallet connected, the token in that wallet, and ETH for gas.

SparkLend is a DeFi lending protocol, and its repay dialog is a useful example because it lets you choose your wallet balance as the payment source. It also shows the new health factor—the measure of how much collateral backs your debt—before you confirm.

Steps to repay a DeFi loan

Follow these steps on the protocol dashboard to reduce or clear your balance.

  1. Open the dashboard with your borrowing wallet connected. The account page should show your borrowed asset and outstanding balance.
  2. Select the borrowed asset and choose Repay. The repay form should identify the debt you are paying down.
  3. Choose your wallet balance as the source. The form should show how much of the borrowed token is available to use.
  4. Enter an amount for a partial repayment, or choose max to pay in full. A partial payment reduces the debt and raises the health factor. For a full payoff, max is the safer choice because Aave V3-style pools accept a maximum-value repay instruction to clear your own debt, avoiding a leftover dust balance.
  5. Approve the token spending cap slightly above the displayed debt. Variable debt grows every second as the reserve’s variable borrow index updates. That means the displayed balance can rise between signing and transaction inclusion. An approval set to exactly the shown debt may fall short; a small buffer covers the change, while max repays only the exact debt.
  6. Confirm the repay transaction and check the account again. The borrow balance should show zero after a full payoff or the lower amount after a partial payment, and the health factor should have risen. That confirms the debt has changed. When closing the position, repay first: withdrawing collateral while debt remains is limited by the health factor. Once the debt is cleared, the collateral can be withdrawn.

Related

Can you repay a DeFi loan partially?

Yes. Enter the amount you want to repay in the borrowed token and confirm the transaction. The remaining balance should be lower, and the health factor should rise.

What happens if you never repay a DeFi loan?

The debt keeps accruing variable interest, and the health factor can fall as the balance grows. If it falls far enough, collateral may be liquidated under the protocol’s rules. Check the position after repayment so you can see whether debt remains and how much collateral is still available to withdraw.
