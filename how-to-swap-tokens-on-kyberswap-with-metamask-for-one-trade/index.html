How to Swap Tokens on KyberSwap With MetaMask for One Trade

Learn how to swap tokens on KyberSwap with MetaMask: connect your wallet, approve an ERC-20 when asked, confirm the trade, and find the new token.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 23, 2026 · 15:41 UTC3 min read

KyberSwap lets you complete a same-chain token swap with MetaMask and see the received token in your wallet. Its aggregator finds routes across supported liquidity sources, giving you a way to compare a route for your token pair before you confirm it with best price swap. MetaMask connects your wallet, signs the transactions and pays gas, the network fee.

How to swap tokens on KyberSwap with MetaMask

Have ready: MetaMask funded on a supported network, the token you want to sell, and enough of that network’s native coin to pay gas. You can’t complete the swap without gas, even when selling a different token.

  1. Open the KyberSwap site and connect MetaMask. Choose Connect Wallet, then MetaMask, and approve the connection in the wallet. The site is connected to your wallet, but a connection alone does not make a trade.
  2. Choose the network and tokens, then enter an amount. Select the network for this same-chain swap, pick the token to sell and the token to receive, and enter how much to trade. The displayed route and trade details give you something to review before signing.
  3. Approve the token if asked. An ERC-20 approval lets the KyberSwap router spend the token you are selling. This is a separate transaction: review it and confirm it in MetaMask. A first sale of an ERC-20 usually means two MetaMask confirmations in total—one for approval, then one for the swap. Selling a native coin such as ETH needs no token approval, so it takes one confirmation for the swap.
  4. Review the trade and confirm the swap. Look at the rate and Minimum Received, the minimum amount of the new token the trade is expected to deliver. Then click Swap and confirm the transaction in MetaMask. The swap is complete when the transaction finishes and the new token appears in your wallet.

Say you are selling an ERC-20 for the first time to get another token. The approval gives the router permission to use the token for that trade; confirming the later swap carries out the exchange. Keeping those two confirmations distinct helps you know what each MetaMask prompt is asking you to authorize.

Related questions

How do I add a swapped token to MetaMask?

MetaMask may not show a token automatically. Import it using the token’s contract address for the network where you received it. Once imported, its balance can appear in your wallet.

Which networks can I swap on with KyberSwap?

KyberSwap supports multiple networks, but availability can vary. Choose a network shown as supported on the site, and make sure MetaMask is on that same network before you select tokens.

Quick answers

Is KyberSwap safe to connect to MetaMask?

A wallet connection lets a site interact with your wallet, while a transaction requires a separate signature. Check that you have opened the intended site and read each MetaMask prompt before approving or swapping.

Why did my KyberSwap swap fail?

A swap can fail if the transaction cannot complete under its current conditions or if it lacks enough gas. Review the transaction details and wallet balance, then try again if the route and amount still suit you.
