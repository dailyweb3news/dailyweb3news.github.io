Visa adds Monad for stablecoin settlement

Visa has added Monad as a settlement option for participating issuers and acquirers, giving them another way to settle card obligations in stablecoins onchain.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 11, 2026 · 08:14 UTC2 min read

Visa has added the Monad blockchain as a stablecoin settlement option for participating card issuers and acquirers, giving them another way to settle obligations using digital tokens designed to hold a steady value. The Monad Foundation’s Nov. 10 announcement quotes a Visa executive confirming the addition.

This concerns settlement between Visa and its financial partners, not a change to how customers pay with their cards. The Foundation says funds can move over Monad on weekends and holidays, when traditional bank settlement typically follows a five-business-day schedule.

How will issuers use Monad?

Issuers and acquirers in Visa’s program can use the network to settle with Visa in stablecoins. Acquirers are banks or payment companies that process card payments for merchants.

Monad is compatible with Ethereum’s software environment, known as the Ethereum Virtual Machine, or EVM. The Foundation says partners already using Ethereum-based custody, wallet and compliance tools can typically extend those systems to Monad instead of building new ones.

The Foundation also says Monad transactions reach finality in under a second. Finality means a transaction has been confirmed and is no longer expected to be reversed. The release describes this as a way to make settlement timing more predictable for acquirers.

Which stablecoins and partners are involved?

The announcement does not name the stablecoins that will be used on Monad or identify participating issuers and acquirers. Unchained’s report on the announcement also notes those details were not provided, and that Visa had not published a separate announcement at the time of its report.

The Foundation’s release says Visa had more than 225 stablecoin-linked card programs live globally as of September 2026. That figure covers Visa’s programs overall; the release does not say how many will use Monad.

When will Monad’s privacy feature be ready?

Not yet: Monad’s planned Private Settlement feature is expected to reach Visa partners in the coming months. The Foundation says it is designed to keep commercially sensitive transaction details private while allowing institutions to share them with auditors and regulators.

The feature is still in development, so its availability and use by Visa partners remain unclear. For now, the confirmed change is that participating partners have another blockchain option for stablecoin settlement.
