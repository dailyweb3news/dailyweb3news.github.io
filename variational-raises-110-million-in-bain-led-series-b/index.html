Variational raises $110 million in Bain-led Series B

Variational raised $110 million at a $1.5 billion valuation, with plans to add markets and lift trading limits as traditional assets drive over half of Omni volume.

The Onchain Brief EditorsNov 21, 2026 · 01:02 UTC2 min read

Variational has raised $110 million in a Series B round led by Bain Capital Crypto, valuing the on-chain derivatives company at $1.5 billion. The company said the funding will help expand its Omni trading platform, add markets and raise limits on the size of open positions. Dragonfly, HSG, Peak XV and 50T Funds also took part, according to Variational’s funding announcement on Business Wire.

Funding targets markets and trading limits

Variational plans to add hundreds of markets and raise open interest caps, which limit the total value of traders’ open positions. The company says traders have increasingly hit those caps. Its Omni platform offers leveraged trading—where traders borrow to increase the size of a position—across more than 550 crypto and traditional markets.

Omni connects traders to liquidity from crypto venues and traditional financial institutions. Its in-house liquidity provider, called OLP, quotes prices across the platform’s markets. The company says OLP earns the spread between quoted prices, allowing Omni to charge no trading fees.

Traditional markets now make up most daily volume

Variational said Omni has processed more than $475 billion in trading volume since launching in early 2025, with more than $1.3 billion in open interest. The platform has over 100,000 accounts, according to the announcement. More than half of its daily volume now comes from traditional markets, such as stocks, indices and commodities.

The company launched “swaps” in September 2026 to bring liquidity from traditional finance to on-chain markets. It said swaps have handled more than $80 billion in volume since launch. These are derivatives that let traders take positions on assets without holding them directly.

Variational plans to expand swaps trading

The company’s plans for the rest of 2026 include adding more swap markets, launching a trading API and moving toward round-the-clock swaps trading. It also plans to expand the network of financial firms that provide prices and liquidity for those markets, the announcement said.

The Block reported that Omni’s traditional markets account for more than half of daily trading volume and that the company is aiming to raise its position limits. The funding adds to a $50 million Series A announced earlier this year, led by Dragonfly, The Block reported.
